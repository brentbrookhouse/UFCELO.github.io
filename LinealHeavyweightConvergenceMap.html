Lineal heavyweight convergence map

I took the heavyweight titles of 15 promotions and followed each one forward the way boxing tracks a lineal champ: the line moves to whoever beats the holder, in any promotion. Every vacancy starts a new version. Of the 26 versions, 21 now end with one man. Each row below is one version, drawn to where its line stands today.

Line passes when the holder loses a heavyweight or openweight MMA bout: title or not, any promotion, by finish, decision or DQ.
Doesn't pass on draws, no-contests, or losses at a lighter weight. Bader's 2020 loss to Nemkov at 205 lb didn't count, for example.
New version each time a promotion fills a vacant belt. Interim belts aren't tracked separately.
Line ends if a holder retires or never loses at heavyweight again.
